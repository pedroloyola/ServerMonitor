ServerAlyzer 1.1.11 makes the Overview and Settings fit better in smaller windows.

Overview. The health bar is never cut off: in a narrow window it moves below the server counts, and its last segment keeps its rounded end.

Pages. When you scroll a page, its content no longer runs under the title bar area.

Settings. The cards stack instead of being cut off when the window is narrow, and Tab and screen readers move through them in the order you see them.

Sidebar. The sidebar is slightly wider, so the names of the pages have more space before they are shortened.
