ServerAlyzer 1.1.8 improves screen reader support and alert reliability.

Screen readers. On the Servers page, a server whose data is out of date now includes how old that data is in the name it gives to screen readers. When a local network search ends on its own, the app now notifies screen readers of how it ended.

Alerts. Fixed a rare case in which an alert could be held back for up to 5 minutes after a server stopped being monitored and was then monitored again.

Portuguese. In the server editor, the example text in the fields now says "Por exemplo:" instead of "Ex.:".
