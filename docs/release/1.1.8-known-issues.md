# ServerAlyzer 1.1.8: known issues

These issues are known in 1.1.8 (package 1.1.8.0) and are **not** fixed in this version. They do not cause data loss and do not block any feature.

## Large text size (Windows text size about 175 % or larger)

With **Settings > Accessibility > Text size** set to a large value (checked at 200 %), some text in ServerAlyzer does not fit its space:

- In **Settings**, the selected language ("Português (Portugal)", "Use system language") and the "Sistema" theme option (Portuguese) can be cut off at the right edge. In high contrast, the highlight of the selected theme option can also be cut off.
- Descriptions that wrap onto two lines can overlap the line above or below them.
- The "restart the app" notice shown after you change the language can be cut off instead of wrapping.

In Settings, every control can still be reached and used with the keyboard, and the full names and values are still exposed to screen readers. In our checks, display scaling (Settings > System > Display > Scale) at 200 % did not cause this.

This is a partial failure of WCAG 2.2 success criterion 1.4.4 (Resize Text). ServerAlyzer 1.1.8 does **not** claim full WCAG 2.2 AA conformance. A fix is planned for 1.1.9.

## Switching between two high contrast themes while the app is open

If you switch from one high contrast theme straight to another (for example Night sky to Desert) while ServerAlyzer is open, the selected item in the sidebar can keep the previous theme's highlight colour, which can be hard to read.

**Workaround:** choose **Exit ServerAlyzer** in the notification area menu, then open ServerAlyzer again.

## Theme choice is not remembered

The Light/Dark choice in Settings applies only until the app is closed. On the next start, ServerAlyzer follows the Windows theme again. Saving the choice is planned for 1.1.9.
